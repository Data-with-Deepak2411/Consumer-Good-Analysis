🚀 From Raw Data to Business Insights | My Latest Project Presentation



I’m excited to share my latest Consumer Goods Analytics project presentation — Codebasics Resume Project Challenge #4 



📊For this challenge, I stepped into the role of a Data Analyst at AtliQ Hardwares, where the objective was simple: turn business questions into meaningful, data-driven answers.



🎯 The Business Challenge

AtliQ Hardwares needed faster and smarter insights to support decision-making in a highly competitive consumer electronics market.

💼 My Mission

Analyze and answer 10 ad-hoc business requests using SQL, Excel, and PowerPoint, transforming raw business data into actionable insights.

🔎 Some Key Discoveries

📈 Product diversity increased significantly — 334 unique products in 2021 vs. 245 in 2020.

💻 The Notebook segment led the portfolio with 129 products.

🚀 The Accessories segment added 34 products, highlighting an opportunity for further growth.

🏷️ Flipkart recorded the highest average pre-invoice discount at 30.83%, indicating an aggressive pricing strategy.

📅 Monthly sales analysis revealed important seasonal patterns and performance fluctuations.

🛒 The Retailer channel contributed 73.22% of gross sales, making it a critical channel for the business.

💡 Business Recommendations

• Expand product variety based on market demand.

• Explore further growth opportunities in the Accessories segment.

• Use seasonal sales patterns to improve forecasting and planning.

• Strengthen and optimize the Retailer channel.

• Monitor discount strategies to balance competitiveness and profitability.

📊 What I Learned

This project strengthened my ability to go beyond writing queries — understanding the business question, analyzing the data, finding the story, and communicating insights that can support better decisions.
